Recorded runs

The aggregates displayed here — both accuracies, the family counts, latency and tokens — are read from each run’s committed report. Call and case outcomes are derived independently from the recorded JSONL, and only to check that the calls and the report still agree: a disagreement stops the app starting rather than quietly changing a number on this page.

Per-case accuracy leads because it is the better estimate of decision quality: it takes the disposition most of a case’s runs reached, once per case, and a tie counts as incorrect. Call-weighted accuracy counts a case once per repeated call, so it depends on which 33 cases this benchmark chose to repeat five times and which 78 it ran once. It is not an estimate of production traffic, and repeated calls measure stability rather than adding independent evidence of accuracy.
