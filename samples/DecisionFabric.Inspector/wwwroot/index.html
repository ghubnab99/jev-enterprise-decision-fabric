Call-weighted accuracy counts a case once per repeated call, so it is a property of this benchmark’s repetition scheme rather than an estimate of production traffic. Repeated calls measure stability and are not independent evidence of accuracy. Per-case accuracy takes the disposition most of a case’s runs reached; a tie counts as incorrect.
